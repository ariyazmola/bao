Understanding Endodontics: Saving Teeth with Root Canal Therapy


Endodontics is a branch of dentistry that focuses on the internal components of the tooth. At the heart of the tooth is the dental pulp, housed in a space called the canal (hence the name "root canal"). When the pulp is damaged, infected, or dead, the nerve damage can cause severe pain.


An endodontic procedure such as a root canal removes the affected pulp instead of removing the tooth. Your dentist will explain the procedure and what steps to take in the days following the procedure.





Our endodontic services:

• Root canal treatment — A procedure to treat a tooth with an infected pulp (nerve).
