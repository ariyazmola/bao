Clear aligners in 8 questions and answers.

  • Home
  • Blog


You’ve probably heard of clear aligners like Invisalign® and SureSmile®—but how do they actually work? How long does treatment take? This article answers these questions and more!


  1. How Do Clear Aligners Work?
    The functioning of aligners like SureSmile® and Invisalign® is quite straightforward. After a detailed 3D scan of your teeth, custom aligners are designed. Their purpose? To snugly fit over your teeth while applying controlled pressure to gradually shift them into the desired position.

    You’ll change aligners weekly (or as recommended), progressively correcting dental arch alignment. Within weeks, you’ll notice visible changes. Your dentist will schedule regular check-ups to monitor progress.


  2. What Are the Advantages of Clear Aligners?
    Clear aligner systems like Invisalign® and SureSmile® offer multiple benefits:

    • Discreet Appearance: Fully transparent for aesthetic appeal at any age
    • Removable Design: Enables unrestricted brushing, flossing, and eating
    • Comfortable Fit: Smooth surfaces prevent irritation
    • Faster Results: Typically achieve desired outcomes quicker than traditional braces



  3. How Long Does Treatment Take?
    The truth? It varies! Treatment duration depends on:

    • Teeth misalignment severity
    • Chosen treatment plan
    • Compliance with wearing instructions

    Only your dentist can provide a personalized timeline after evaluation.



  4. Daily Wear Time Required

    For optimal results, wear aligners 20-22 hours daily, essentially full-time except for meals and oral hygiene.



  5. What’s the Cost of Invisalign® or SureSmile®?

    Pricing reflects the complexity of your case. More significant corrections requiring longer treatment with additional aligners and appointments will increase costs. A professional consultation will give you precise figures.



  6. Is Wearing Aligners Painful?

    While not pain-free, any discomfort is temporary and manageable. Some sensitivity is normal (the mouth is highly innervated!), but the smooth shells and gradual pressure ensure good tolerance.



  7. Who Can Use Clear Aligners?
    Ideal for:

    • Teens (during peak growth periods)
    • Adults (it’s never too late for alignment!)

    Age is no barrier to achieving your dream smile.



  8. Where to Get Personalized Advice?

    Considering clear aligners? Book a consultation at B cabinet Dental, we’ll gladly answer all your questions!
