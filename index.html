  • How much do I have to go to the dental clinic?
    We recommend visiting our dentist in Ahuntsic every 6 months for routine exams and preventive services to maintain optimal oral health and catch problems early.
    Will dental cleanings be painful in any way?
    No! Our gentle touch approach makes dental care comfortable. Regular cleanings prevent gum treatments and keep your natural teeth healthy.
    When and why would I need root canal treatment?
    If you have severe pain from infection, our root canal treatment can save your tooth. We provide quality care to relieve discomfort.
    What solutions is there for one or multiple broken teeth?
    From dental restorations to cosmetic treatments, our dental office provides solutions for damaged teeth that look and feel natural.
    Can you fix my teeth that are discolored?
    Yes! We offer whiter teeth solutions including professional whitening as part of our cosmetic dentistry services in Montreal.
  • What is myotherapy?
    Our myotherapy treatment uses gentle exercises to correct jaw position and relieve jaw pain which is perfect for patients with misaligned teeth or TMJ issues.
    How do you treat my teeth grinding issue?
    We create custom night guards to protect your natural teeth from damage caused by grinding, helping prevent tooth wear and morning jaw pain.
    What’s better for me Invisalign or SureSmile?
    Both Invisalign and SureSmile offer discreet orthodontic treatment. We will recommend the best option for your smile goals during a consultation.
    Can adults also get orthodontic treatment?
    Absolutely! Our cosmetic dentistry services include adult friendly options like clear aligners for a perfect smile at any age.
    Do you handle dental emergencies right away?
    Yes! We offer help for issues like broken aligners or severe jaw pain at our dental office.
