Denture Solutions: Restoring Your Smile with Precision


Dentures may be necessary when one or more teeth need to be replaced, in whole or in part. We cover the cost of care for removable or fixed dentures. These can be placed on natural teeth or implants.


People who wear dentures must receive rigorous monitoring tailored to their specific situation. During your consultation, we will inform you about the daily hygiene measures to adopt to protect your denture and your oral health.





Our prosthodontic services:

• Partials — Dentures that replace one or more teeth.

• Dentures — Complete prostheses that replace all teeth and require special care.

• Crowns — Crowns made and fitted to the teeth to be restored, which completely cover them.

• Bridges — Restorations that replace one or more missing teeth by attaching to the adjacent teeth. They are fixed tooth replacements that are cemented to the teeth that serve as anchors.
